The Florida Pipe Store

By Anthony Varas

Hello, I’m Anthony Varas, a local artist born and raised in Florida. I’m the owner of The Florida Pipe Store. I handcraft small batches of ceramic tobacco pipes, creating unique designs for smoke shops and retailers.

I make my pieces from stoneware clay, finish them with non-toxic, food-safe glazes, and fire them to full vitrification. For tobacco use only.

If you’d like to learn more about my work or are interested in working together, send me an email. I’d love to hear from you.
